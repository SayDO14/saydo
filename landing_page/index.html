From your voice to your calendar in three steps

  1. SpeakTap the mic and talk the way you normally would. French, English or Arabic.
  2. CheckSayDo shows the title, date, time and people it understood. Fix anything before saving.
  3. ConfirmEvents reach Google Calendar. Tasks and emails land in their own lists.

What it can create

EventsAppointments with date, time and people, synced to your Google Calendar.
TasksTo-dos with an optional due date and notes. Tick them off when done.
EmailsSay who and what. You review the message, then approve it before it is sent.
ContactsAddresses are remembered, so next time “email Sarah” takes one tap.

Install in under a minute

  1. 1Open this page on your Android phone and tap Download for Android. If the browser warns about the file, choose Download anyway.
  2. 2Open the downloaded file. If Android asks, allow installs from your browser (Allow from this source), go back and tap Install.
  3. 3If Play Protect says the app is not verified, tap More details, then Install anyway. This appears because SayDo is not on the Play Store yet.

The first voice request can take up to a minute while the server wakes up. After that it answers in seconds.
